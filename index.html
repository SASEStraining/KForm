Kitchen Compliance Audit

A one-question-at-a-time inspection for commercial kitchens, covering food safety & hygiene, premises & equipment, cleaning, pest control, work health & safety, and fire & emergency safety. Built from SA Health, Food Standards Code (Standards 3.2.2 & 3.2.3), and SafeWork SA guidance.

Grading scale — used for every question
0 Fully compliant — no issues observed
1 Minor observation — best-practice suggestion only
2 Minor non-conformance — low risk, monitor
3 Moderate non-conformance — corrective action required
4 Major non-conformance — high risk, urgent action
5 Critical failure — immediate risk, stop activity
An in-progress audit was found on this device.

This tool supports a visual self-inspection and is not a substitute for a formal audit, a registered food safety auditor, or advice from SA Health / SafeWork SA. Reference standards may be updated — always confirm current requirements with the relevant authority.
